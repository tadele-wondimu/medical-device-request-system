Medical Device Knowledge Base

Emergency Category

Defibrillator
Delivers electric shocks to restore normal heart rhythm during cardiac emergencies
Crash Cart
Mobile unit containing emergency medications and resuscitation equipment
Ventilator
Provides mechanical breathing support for patients with respiratory failure
EKG/ECG Machine
Records electrical activity of the heart to detect cardiac abnormalities
Infusion Pump
Precisely delivers fluids, medications, or nutrients intravenously

Imaging Category

X-ray Machine
Uses radiation to produce images of internal body structures
CT Scanner
Combines X-ray images from different angles to create cross-sectional views
MRI Machine
Uses magnetic fields and radio waves to create detailed organ images
Ultrasound Machine
Uses sound waves to visualize internal organs and blood flow
PET Scan
Nuclear imaging technique that shows metabolic processes in the body

Surgical Instruments

Electrosurgical Unit
Uses high-frequency current for cutting tissue and controlling bleeding
Anesthesia Machine
Delivers precise mix of medical gases and anesthetic agents
Surgical Laser
Provides precise cutting and cauterization during operations
Endoscope
Flexible tube with camera for internal examinations and surgeries

Patient Monitoring

Patient Monitor
Tracks vital signs including heart rate, blood pressure, and oxygen levels
Pulse Oximeter
Measures oxygen saturation in blood through light absorption
Fetal Monitor
Tracks fetal heart rate and uterine contractions during childbirth
Telemetry System
Wireless monitoring of cardiac patients' heart rhythms

Laboratory Equipment

Centrifuge
Separates blood components through high-speed rotation
Microscope
Enables examination of microscopic specimens and cells
Blood Analyzer
Automates complete blood count (CBC) and other blood tests
Autoclave
Sterilizes equipment using high-pressure saturated steam

Physical Therapy

TENS Unit
Provides pain relief through electrical nerve stimulation
Continuous Passive Motion Machine
Automatically moves joints post-surgery to prevent stiffness
Ultrasound Therapy Machine
Uses sound waves for deep tissue treatment and pain relief
Hydrotherapy Pool
Provides buoyancy-assisted exercise in warm water
